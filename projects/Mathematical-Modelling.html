Route Optimisation, a Mathematical Comparison of Bezier Curves and Linear Interpolation

Investigated the implementation of Bezier curves for autonomous vehicles in an aircraft’s autopilot system, as a form of route optimization to reduce the aerodynamic load on the aircraft and increase the comfort of passengers by reducing bank angles and reducing the curvature of the aircraft during its intended route. This was done by modelling complex fly-by and fly-over waypoints, that were then plotted on geogebra over the imprint of the map. The results were compared to a parametric best fit function, where a comparative second-derivative curvature analysis was completed. The results demonstrated a clear lower curvature observed in Bezier Curves, providing gentler turning radii and hence creating smoother, more aerodynamically efficient manoeuvres.
