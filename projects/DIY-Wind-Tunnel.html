Improvised Aerodynamics, Exploring the Relationship Between Airspeed and Lift Using a DIY Wind Tunnel.

Engineered and designed a wind tunnel, making use of cardboard and a variable wind speed fan, to investigate the relationship between airflow freestream velocity and lift forces generated. A physical aerofoil was created out of cardstock paper and supported on a weighing scale to measure the change in weight through a series of controlled changing airspeeds. Lift outputs were measured over different flow rates, providing an opportunity to test and observe theoretical fluid dynamics through low-cost rapid prototyping.
